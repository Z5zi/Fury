# Ofc. Renn reference study and individual construction criteria

Dedicated study began 7 October 2026 at 00:21:22 UTC. The actual official
Rockstar [GTA VI screenshot gallery](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media/screenshots)
and downloaded Jason Duval 03/04 and Boobie Ike 03 pixels were inspected for
Renn. The source URLs, hashes and viewing status are in `REFERENCE_LEDGER.json`.
Reference images stay private and are not source-package or game assets.

## Readable form under interior light

Boobie Ike 03 has broad cheek transitions, recessed eyelids and a lower lid that
continues around the socket. The ear has a recessed concha and distinct outer
rim. Neck volume expands toward the shoulder rather than ending in a cylinder.
Colored room light changes highlights and shadows without turning the base
complexion into a lighter identity. His face, beard, jewelry, surface textures,
clothing design, pose and likeness are not copied. Renn has his own independent
18-target anatomical recipe, clean face, uniform cap and exact seeded palette.

Jason 03/04 demonstrate connected finger webbing, a real thumb base, a cuff
that surrounds the wrist, and an upper sleeve attached along an armhole.
Cloth tension gathers near the bend and fastening points; quiet larger panels
keep the garment silhouette readable. The shirt design, prints, cap design,
logos and likeness are not copied. Renn retains the original broad security
shoulders and a full protective vest with the existing badge, radio and pouch.

## Renn-specific targets

- Give the broader face a distinct moderate squared jaw, fuller cheek transition,
  broader nose and less chin projection than Hale. Fit eyelids and globes to
  Renn's own source seams and retain the dark linear complexion `(0.35, 0.205,
  0.14)` in the appearance record
- Reserve cap clearance above the source crown without changing the exact
  1.82 m nominal height. The opaque short hair must cover the complete scalp,
  while the cap has a band, a compact finite-thickness peak and restrained seams
- Broaden the ribcage and shoulders while keeping a more restrained waist.
  The vest needs complete rear/side volume, shoulder bridges and armholes;
  a smooth front rendering cannot substitute for a closed all-side garment
- Fit connected hands to independently placed wrist joints. Keep the original
  animations and controller inputs; this is static rest-shape fitting, not a
  claim of finger animation or new gameplay abilities
- Give the trousers one connected rise/seat and both legs, then fit service
  footwear with a heel, sole, toe and supported laces. Keep the exact sole plane
- Preserve actual bank scenery and the original scene/controller behavior in
  world evidence. Renn's existing spawn lies in the inner wall; the prior
  navigation audit documents that defect. It is not corrected by this art pass

## Acceptance and limits

Inspect eight face and body directions, both LODs, neutral clay, attention turns,
run/crouch and actual bank light. Use renderer/source/contact tests as evidence
against failures rather than as a claim of visual parity. This prototype lacks
production subsurface skin, expression/blink animation, strand hair physics,
cloth simulation, high-detail facial maps and production motion capture. The
reference quality is a direction to study; neither the two-hour allocation nor
passing numerical checks establishes GTA VI/AAA parity.
