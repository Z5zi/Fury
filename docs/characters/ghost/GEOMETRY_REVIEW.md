## Shape and identity

The original model has a long primitive face, separate nose/eye/ear pieces, mitten hands, narrow segmented neck, a front-only pair of cyan vest slabs and an open rectangular patch in the rear hair. The final model has a continuous source cheek–jaw–neck silhouette, complete mouth and exterior chin, fitted source eyes, source-connected fingers, a broader relaxed torso, a complete lined identification vest and covered scalp/nape. The broad jaw, forward brow and moderate projecting nose belong to the separately authored Ghost recipe. Neither a reference person's likeness nor another Fury actor's head was used as the result.

The original rust shirt, plum trousers, chestnut hair and cyan role tint remain recognizable in ordinary world frames. The pale identification band now wraps the actual rear and sides. Its horizontal sewing rows are real vest facets, so they remain on the vest during movement rather than drifting across a separately skinned surface.

## Reference-guided corrections

The saved Rockstar Jason and Lucia material, Sorici portrait/eye/hair construction, Widelski garment study and Unreal material/hair examples were used as quality criteria. Continuous anatomy, recessed eyes, fitted garment boundaries and coherent hair growth are the relevant observations. The final model is still a bounded procedural game asset, with far simpler materials, groom and facial performance than those professional references.

Actual path-traced head views caught a cap-like matte mass with one conspicuous diagonal lock. A glossy material experiment was rejected: it produced a plastic highlight and failed real card-facing/winding checks. The accepted groom keeps an opaque matte scalp, mild asymmetric forward volume, shallow combed relief and 80 fitted crest cards plus 64 shorter edge cards at Near. Far retains six crest and ten edge cards. All use the existing short-strand response at the narrow card scale. Reducing the physical mid-card lift removed an obvious dotted shadow stripe in the path view. The result is a modest short swept groom; it is not strand simulation or an anisotropic hair shader.

Close hand/waist views exposed a real defect that full-body sheets hid: the old waistband ellipse protruded through the shirt, creating dark side bars and triangular rear patches, especially in Far. The waistband now follows the narrower trouser opening beneath the shirt. Near/Far front, rear, side and moving oblique images show a clean shirt break. The contact audit was strengthened to include shirt–trouser crossings, with two additional real-triangle corruption controls. No assertion was weakened to accept the result.

The earlier ordinary-run boot/cloth clash was corrected with an open boot shaft, turned cuff, projected tongue/laces, a raised trouser break and one local ankle influence field shared by the cloth cuff, boot and underlay. The shared animation/controller was not changed. Final ordinary run/walk, bind, idle, talk and attention scopes pass for both LODs at all three test heights.

## Face, hands and movement

Both LODs preserve the same source face anatomy and complete source mouth. Combined extreme attention images were checked from both sides; the lower lip, chin and exterior jaw follow the head continuously. The actual model test checks all 4,125 canonical head/mouth/socket positions through 24 combined poses, including all 418 mouth and 3,185 exterior-head positions. This is full source Head motion, not lip-sync or a new jaw animation system.

The wrists share exact source boundary positions and influences with their connected forearms. The Near hands retain anatomical finger separation; Far is visibly simpler in deliberately close diagnostic views. Fingers have a restrained static rest curl, without new animated finger bones. Ordinary arm swing remains the production motion.

The final pose sheets include full crouch and crouch-walk. These are stress diagnostics: the received protocol does not carry a crouch state. Full crouch-walk still produces implausibly folded legs and cloth/thigh contact under the shared animation. Strict tests retain the first shirt/thigh failure and earlier vest/thigh witnesses. These failures are not covered by the ordinary-motion pass and are not described as clean.

## Actual network world views

The final synthetic peers send real production-format UDP snapshots to NetClient. Walking distance, direction changes, heat/cash modulation, accepted empty rosters, disconnect and same/different-peer return reach the production remote presentation block. No received state is replaced by direct transform assignment. The original/new art comparison uses identical lifecycle code and packet/input schedule; only Ghost profile dispatch differs.

The forward camera shows clear ordinary cyan silhouette and arm/leg movement, but a pole and moving vehicle later occlude the proxy. Those original scene actors and props are retained. A reverse camera gives unobstructed empty-roster and return views, including the complete cyan back and stripe. Other people may cross the view normally.

Heat/cash values, material albedo and scalar emission remain exactly equal to the original model's state on matching frames. The existing textured material responds with stronger whitening than the old untextured primitive model under the same emission. This visible material-response difference is disclosed; the shared shader and modulation equations were not altered. Ordinary palette preservation does not claim pixel equivalence during the flash.

## Limits

Near is 39,346 triangles; Far is 15,970. Face anatomy is prioritized over Far cloth/hair smoothness, so the deliberately enlarged Far head has visible groom facets. There is no new skin subsurface model, anisotropic hair, facial blend-shape performance, cloth simulation or collision/controller redesign. Existing sub-micron cache seam rounding is documented separately from visible garment defects. No AAA or GTA VI parity is claimed.

The enlarged final run phase 0.1875 side views retain a flared cloth break over the lifted boot. The ordinary intersection audit passes, but its deformation is still a simple weighted cloth approximation under the shared level-foot solver. The clearance fix does not establish natural cloth simulation. The final night-world close-up retains seated eyes, the full jaw and cyan vest; the distant actual Far view preserves the cyan/stripe cue while suppressing fine groom and finger detail.
