## First actual rendered critique

The exact baseline has a front-only pair of cyan slabs with a horizontal pale
stripe. The rear is plain shirt, the neck is a narrow cylinder, facial features
are separate small primitives, and the rear hair has a rectangular gap.

WIP01 adds a complete independently fitted vest/back, fuller torso, source head,
connected hands, open cuffs and shaped shoes. Material and clay front/back/side
images were inspected. The head has a distinct broad jaw, forward brow and
moderate nose projection. Its original rust/plum/chestnut/cyan identity reads
at full-body distance. WIP01 still shows an overly short cap-like hair mass and
blocky equal-length fringe. The next iteration adds swept directional mass,
shortens exposed fringe tips and refits actual cap-supported ribbons. The
shoulder/armhole and trousers/waist need pose/contact review; initial stills alone
do not establish their fit during movement.
