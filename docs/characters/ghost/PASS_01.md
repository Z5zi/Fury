## Integration and limits

`ghost-evidence/integration/README.md` identifies owned paths and small shared
registration/fixture deltas. The separately authorized lifecycle repair and
opt-in network diagnostics have distinct patches. No main-tree edits, public
publication, GitHub writes, external server or user-PC work occurred.

The reference-led quality improvements are documented in `GEOMETRY_REVIEW.md`.
Remaining limits are full-crouch cloth/thigh contact, a simplified flared cloth
break under the shared level-foot solver, coarse Far groom/finger detail,
no facial blend-shape performance or anisotropic hair/skin subsurface model,
and stronger whitening of the new textured material under unchanged numeric
cash/heat emission. No AAA or GTA VI parity is claimed.

`PERFORMANCE.md` gives the measured CPU/memory cost. Warm pose medians are
1.0833–1.3893 ms Near and 0.4174–0.4329 ms Far on one cloud CPU. The 120-frame final
world capture took 36.009 s including startup and capture; simulation FPS is not
live frame rate. The evidence is the accepted-eight base plus Ghost, not a
performance claim for the coordinator's eventual full roster.
