# Cass Vesper: reference study and first fitting criteria

Studied 7 October 2026 UTC for `NpcFence`. This document records actual viewed
pixels and original authoring goals, not a completed visual acceptance.

## Canonical identity

Cass is the Ashcourt fence contact, displayed as **Cass Vesper**, gameplay
`NpcKind::Fence`, appearance `CharacterRole::Fence`, height **1.78 m**,
stable FNV-1a seed **0x513cdaa9**. The authored route, 1.2 m/s speed,
day-only schedule, shop and discretion/trading dialogue remain authoritative.
The model pass changes no location, collision, navigation or shop behavior.

The inherited appearance has skin `(0.90,0.69,0.52)`, auburn hair
`(0.27,0.11,0.052)`, teal jacket `(0.23,0.39,0.37)`, gray-green trousers
`(0.19,0.23,0.20)`, mauve accent `(0.43,0.32,0.45)`, and dark footwear
`(0.075,0.085,0.095)`. These are linear colors. The old builder's shoulder,
waist and face multipliers are 0.94, 1.00 and 1.02. Its swept hair, dark
glasses and mauve scarf/lapels are identity anchors. These observations do not
establish age, ethnicity, gender identity, wealth or extra affiliations.

## Actually viewed official GTA VI references

Source: [Rockstar's official screenshot gallery](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media/screenshots).
The three following 3840×2160 original files were downloaded through their
official gallery links and individually inspected in the image viewer. They
were displayed at 2048×1152; no claim of inspecting all source pixels is made.

- [Raul Bautista 01](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/_next/static/media/Raul_Bautista_01.0md1ii-yrn96r.jpg?akim=1&imdensity=1&imwidth=3840):
  three-quarter glasses bridge seating, separated lens/rim depth, temple
  continuation toward the ear, and swept hair with a continuous dark root
  mass. The finger pads bend around the phone rather than ending as tubes.
  Cass should have a believable face behind the glasses and a continuous
  scalp under the swept top. Raul's likeness, facial hair, expression, phone,
  jewelry and longer rear hair are not Cass assets or requirements.
- [Raul Bautista 04](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/_next/static/media/Raul_Bautista_04.0qx77gyvykvv5.jpg?akim=1&imdensity=1&imwidth=3840):
  the light jacket's lapels have a clear roll above the chest and a notch
  where collar and lapel meet. Broad shoulder and chest planes stay quiet;
  folds collect at elbow and underarm. A pocket welt is a thin attached strip,
  not a bulky pouch. The reference is colored nightlife lighting, unsuitable
  for copying neutral cloth or complexion values. Cass keeps the teal and
  mauve identity and an original jacket construction.
- [Boobie Ike 01](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/_next/static/media/Boobie_Ike_01.0-wji2pg5anfs.jpg?akim=1&imdensity=1&imwidth=3840):

  tattoos, cash, portrait identity and implied status are excluded.

These proprietary images remain private reference material. No geometry,
texture, pattern, likeness or logo from them is copied into Fury or shipped.
GTA VI is a visual study benchmark, not a claimed fidelity level.

## Original construction choices to test

- Distinct softly angular cheeks, moderate straight nose, broader chin plane
  and separately positioned ears, derived from a reviewed CC0 recipe
- Swept auburn top with continuous side/nape coverage, a restrained offset
  part and short edge cards; no bald rear band or disconnected hair blocks
- Dark rounded-rectangular lenses, closed rims, seated nose bridge/pads and
  complete temples with curved ear ends; glasses remain in both LODs
- Narrow structured teal shoulders, fitted sleeves, actual rolled mauve lapels,
  a scarf lying around the real neck and descending beneath the lapel opening
- Quiet waist shaping, rear seam/vent and weighted turned hem; shallow welt
  pockets rather than an invented tactical or luxury wardrobe
- Connected source hands in an independently fitted relaxed rest shape,
  shaped trouser seat/knees and dark boots with a separate sole and instep

Acceptance needs actual body/head pixels from eight directions, Near/Far,
material/clay, attention and locomotion poses, and unaltered shop scenery.
Face/finger performance, cloth/hair simulation and scanned skin detail are
not supplied by this construction method and must remain disclosed.
