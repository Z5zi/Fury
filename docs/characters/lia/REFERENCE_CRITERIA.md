## Original Lia criteria

Preserve stable identity `NpcTeller`, display name Lia Merrow, BankStaff role,
1.68 m height, seed `327b8688`, original complexion and near-black swept hair.
The role supplies a pale service shirt `(0.62, 0.66, 0.68)`, pointed collar,
narrow tie and name badge, with no bag or protective vest. Neither the seeded
identity nor this art direction establishes a biography or demographic claims.

The proposed head has a broader forehead/cheek envelope and a taper into a
compact chin, a restrained nose tip and distinct mouth/lid proportions. It must
be visibly different from Mira's oval face, Tessa's compact round face and
Owen's narrower long head. Fit eyes against Lia's own retained socket seam,
not another character's helper offset. Keep the exact selected source faces,
UV charts, complete attachment loop and a joined hidden lower-neck underlay.

Build a slightly roomy, tucked service shirt with its own body proportions,
sewn shoulder openings/sleeves, collar stand and pointed leaves, cuff turn,
placket, buttons, narrow tie and fitted badge. Her broader original waist
should remain readable. Use short dark sides/nape and a directional swept
crown, with continuous base coverage and sparse tapered cards. The dark hair
must not become a recolored close crop or inflated radial helmet.

Review all sides, top/low views, neutral material, both LODs and motion before
claiming success. Show real bank-counter captures with authored scenery and
live AI retained. A laboratory portrait alone is not game integration proof.

Additional actual shirt views inspected at 19:18 UTC include the maker's full
front with/without tie and cuff close-up (exact URLs/hashes in the manifest).
These expose shoulder slope, a continuous tucked rise, the cuff placket and
buttons, and how a narrow knot sits between collar leaves. The first broad,
tube-like shirt was rejected against those construction criteria.

[William Paré-Jobin's character construction breakdown, published by Marmoset](https://marmoset.co/posts/real-time-character-design-creation-and-presentation-breakdown/)
was read and diagrams 06, 07 and 08 individually inspected at 19:28 UTC. They
separate continuous scalp coverage from extra cards, show different growth
around the crown/ears, and show shallow card curvature. The original hair,
beard, fantasy costume and tools' shaders are not copied. Lia's first smooth
cap was rejected as insufficiently swept; the own-mesh groom is being revised.
