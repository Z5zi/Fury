## Independent original art direction

Metro Watch remains a unit label, not an invented personal name or biography.
Keep the existing skin and auburn hair palettes; uniform shirt is linear RGB
(0.16, 0.22, 0.28), trousers (0.095, 0.13, 0.175), cap (0.10, 0.155, 0.21),
vest (0.08, 0.12, 0.165), equipment (0.055, 0.065, 0.075), badge
(0.64, 0.68, 0.53). The new body is fitted to the original narrow shoulder/waist
ratios 0.93/0.91 and the 1.82 m gameplay height.

The distinct recipe uses an oval head, narrower temple-to-jaw silhouette,
smaller tapered chin, restrained shallow nose, quiet brow and limited ear
rotation. None of the graphical parameter labels establishes ethnicity, age,
gender identity or personal history. It must remain distinguishable from Hale,
Renn, Vale and Owen before cap/equipment decoration.

Priorities: a connected head/neck and hand mesh; fitted auburn hairline and
nape under a finite cap band; curved visor with an underside; narrower sewn
uniform shoulders/sleeves; a real front/back vest shell with open armholes;
seated radio/badge/belt hardware; trousers with knee/seat shaping; separate
boot uppers and soles. Study all eight body/head directions in material and
clay, attention and crouching, near/far and actual dynamic gameplay.
