# Sgt. Hale reference and review criteria

This is a first, individual prototype-art pass for `NpcGuard`, not a claim of matching GTA VI or a commercial character asset. The preserved palette, nominal 1.85 m height, security cap, protective vest, badge, radio and hip pouch are the existing character identity. No age, ethnicity, scars, rank insignia, department or biography is inferred from the source controls or references.

## Actual reference study

Rockstar's official Jason Duval screenshots 03 and 04 were viewed as full photographic-style game images, including a renewed actual-pixel inspection on 7 October. The observations used here are continuous head/ear/neck surfaces, volume around the eye and nose, a cloth collar that folds over a supported yoke, garment tension at shoulder/elbow, and complete fingers rather than mitten attachments. The subject's likeness, patterned shirt, tattoos, jewelry and pose were not copied. Hale has his own documented anatomical morph recipe, uniform and fixed hand rest.

Guillaume Ds's Ubisoft security-guard body and close-up portfolio images were viewed for supported equipment, separate textile/rigid material response, protective garment thickness and rear/side assembly. Its fictional armored helmet, branding, symbols, forms and textures are not part of Hale.

Private reference pixels are excluded from source and evidence archives. Reference URLs and original-image hashes are retained in the private study, while this document lists the public source pages:

- https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media/screenshots
- https://guillaumeds.artstation.com/projects/g8Z4XG

## Review outcomes to establish

1. Front, ±45°, ±90°, ±135° and back must show a connected shirt/yoke/sleeves, complete neck/head, supported folded collar, full vest back and straps, cap peak/crown/band, nape and ears, complete hands, trousers and footwear. Use identical camera/light and retain every view, including weaker ones
2. The cap must fit the actual source scalp; the shorter original visor must keep its silhouette without inventing an unshadowed face. Direct ray and path images are checked separately from raster, which does not provide the same cast shadow
3. Fabric/equipment must stay attached during both gait extremes, attention limits, crouch and crouch-walk. These are diagnostic samples of the existing animator, not proof that this guard performs all those actions in gameplay
4. Real-world stills and motion must retain the original bank scene and player-triggered responses. Do not hide walls, disable guards, adjust heat or relocate Hale for a cleaner picture
5. The known bank guard navigation faults remain outside this art pass. Passing model contact tests cannot certify obstacle collision or navigation
6. Near/Far preserve the same source head and character identity. Reduced hand and cloth support must still be inspected as actual rendered geometry, rather than accepted from counts

## Visible limitations, still applicable

The shared renderer/material system remains a prototype. Skin lacks subsurface scattering and a commercial facial texture set; there is no facial-expression, eye-tracking, finger or cloth simulation. Hair is a short opaque scalp with masked boundary cards, not individual simulated strands or an anisotropic groom. The guard model cannot repair the existing wall/prop clipping in patrol and chase. All those limits remain visible in the delivered evidence.
