# Mira Vale geometry and deformation review

Review date: 2026-10-06. This review exercises the actual `NpcCivA` / `Commuter`
profile generated by `make_character_profile`; it does not substitute the legacy
humanoid, a renderer proxy, or hand-written sample triangles.

## Regression coverage

`tests/character_profile_tests.cpp` checks both near and far models at 1.35 m,
1.75 m (Mira's actual roster height), and 2.10 m:

- Every index is in range; every face has three distinct indices, positive area,
  and winding consistent with its vertex normals
- Positions, unit normals, tint, opacity, and two-bone influences are finite and
  valid; all 17 rig joints influence actual vertices
- UVs remain inside the content area of one of the ten original atlas regions,
  and no triangle crosses a material-region boundary; head triangles cannot
  sweep across the cylindrical U wrap inside the Face region
- Both soles touch the exact ground plane at rest; the head-to-ground height and
  body depth/width remain plausible, with positive signed shell volume
- Both LODs retain the same rig, seed, appearance, shared PBR atlas, and individual
  switch distance; the reduced mesh removes more than half the geometry
- Both LODs preserve the MPFB-derived anatomical head's source positions and
  triangle winding and original per-corner UVs; duplicate seam vertices retain
  identical normals and skin weights without changing shape
- Head/neck pivots match the morphed source landmarks; both actual eyeballs match
  source eye centers and helper sizes and share the skull's transform
- Repeated construction is bit-deterministic
- 1,188 actual sampled and skinned poses cover idle, walk, run, talk with
  attention, crouch, and crouch-walk: 33 frames per state, three heights, two LODs
- Every sampled vertex stays finite with a unit normal; every sampled triangle
  retains positive area, actual shoe vertices stay above the ground, and adjacent
  frames remain bounded
- Skinning retains vertex/index storage, index order, mesh identity, renderer
  handles, tint, UVs, and opacity; applying an identical pose does not dirty it
  twice; the bind mesh remains unchanged
- 192 fallback cases compare every geometry, rig, weight, appearance, and identity
  field bit for bit with `make_character_model`, covering the other current
  roster identities, dynamic guard/enforcer spawns, player, ghost, unknown and
  case-mismatched names, embedded NULs, and `NpcCivA` used with other roles
- Invalid heights, roles, and LODs fail explicitly for both individual and
  generic requests

Float bit comparisons intentionally ignore C++ structure padding and runtime
mesh/GPU identity counters: those are not authored geometry or identity data.
The signed volume check sums overlapping authored shells; it is not a claim
that clothing, eyes, and the body form a single watertight boolean union.

## CPU geometry budget

The reviewed asset caps are 40,000 near triangles and 16,000 far triangles,
with a greater-than-50% reduction in both vertices and triangles at the far LOD.
These are asset-local ceilings, not a frame-rate promise or a world-wide NPC
budget. Runtime cost also includes skinning, ray acceleration updates, shadow
rays, resolution, and all other visible geometry. The legacy generic character's
2,500-triangle ceiling does not apply to this dedicated close-range asset.

The first draft measured 59,512 near and 16,798 far triangles (35,110 / 11,098
vertices). Although its 71.8% triangle reduction was meaningful, both LODs
exceeded the initial individual budget. The earlier procedural-head revision
used 39,662 near and 9,344 far triangles. That was a numerical checkpoint, not a
claim that its facial quality was sufficient.

After image review, the anatomical head changed to the intact 8,824-triangle
MPFB-derived surface. Both LODs retain this topology to preserve eyelids, lip
loops, nostrils, ears, and silhouette. The far cap is explicitly raised from
10,000 to 16,000 for this reviewed asset; the near cap and the requirement to
remove more than half the overall vertices and triangles remain unchanged. The
Face-density reduction assertion is replaced with source-geometry preservation;
hair, skin details, jacket, and leather must still lose substantial geometry.

This is a bounded quality/cost tradeoff. A later reduced head should be authored
with feature constraints and pass real image comparisons before replacing the
intact surface. Numerical triangle removal alone would not establish that.

## Defects identified during review

1. Fractional powers of a float sine at curve endpoints produced NaNs in eyelids
   and lips. Clamping the sine to its intended nonnegative domain removed them
2. Collapsing every hair-crown point to the same position produced degenerate
   faces. A finite, shaped crown radius removed that collapse
3. The engine's general `normalize` zero-vector threshold discarded valid tiny
   cap normals on nails, eye details, and the mouth seam. At 1.75 m, the corrected
   endpoint geometry still had 664 near and 186 far zero normals. The authored
   sculpt's scale-safe normal normalization fixes these without weaker tests
4. Far hair tubes twisted at an abrupt tangent-reference change. Nine genuine
   winding/normal disagreements remained after excluding zero-normal mouth caps;
   continuous transported frames fix these curves
5. Reducing the thin shoulder bridge to six sides produced four far-only
   winding/normal disagreements. Retaining eight sides in that small component
   removed all four, costing only 20 extra far triangles
6. The initial anatomical integration assigned cylindrical UVs to welded source
   vertices, so 128 face triangles interpolated across the U wrap. They remained
   inside the Face atlas rectangle but swept across its content. A focused
   regression now checks this previously uncovered seam condition. Original
   per-corner source UVs, duplicated seam vertices and shared normal groups fix
   the issue without altering source positions or triangle topology
7. A later rounded-crown pass introduced 86 near and 16 far winding/normal
   disagreements in shallow four-sided hair locks. Eight-sided cross sections
   remove all of them while preserving the centerlines, radii and flattening.
   The final model remains within the existing 40,000 / 16,000 triangle caps

The initial deformation-only diagnostic passed all 1,188 poses once the NaNs and
crown collapse were repaired. It temporarily omitted the independently known
normal and static-geometry failures to expose any additional deformation issues.
That diagnostic is not the complete regression pass: the repository test keeps
all checks, including normals, winding, and budgets, mandatory.

## Corrective follow-up: 2026-10-06

The preceding first-pass evidence remains a historical checkpoint. A related
Jon deformation review exposed a coverage gap: positive triangle area and
finite skinned normals do not prove that a crouched trouser mesh does not
intersect itself. Mira inherited the same narrow knee-blend construction, so
this follow-up intentionally changes her deformation and posterior leg shape.
It is not described as byte-identical to her earlier geometry.

The applied correction enables dual-quaternion skinning, blends shin/thigh
weights over normalized Y `[-0.325, -0.125]`, and adds a posterior trouser Z
offset of `0.013 * exp(-((y + 0.225) / 0.20)^2) * max(0, -sin(angle))`.
Jon's initial 0.012h offset still produced a proper crossing on Mira's denser
mesh at crouch-walk frame 9, between triangles 13750 and 14278. Raising only
Mira's offset to 0.013h cleared the full sampled set. This adds at most 1.75 mm
over the first corrective candidate at her 1.75 m roster height; it does not
increase the mesh density or the existing budgets.

The earlier structural split was independently compared against the preserved
pre-split source and matched all authored fields exactly. Later periodic
circumference/endpoint repairs and shared Face-chart packing are also intentional
changes, separate from that comparison. Canonical anatomical source arrays and
ordered head triangles remain preserved. Mira uses opaque modeled hair and
has no HairStrands cards.

The current `tests/character_profile_tests.cpp` additionally verifies:

- Closed Pants shells after welding only rounding-scale seam drift, with two
  oppositely directed triangles per geometric edge
- Proper nonadjacent knee triangle intersections at rest and in **all 594**
  sampled run/crouch/crouch-walk poses across three heights and both LODs
- Every optional third influence/index/weight and the model's skinning-mode flag
  in normalization, bone-usage, determinism and generic-fallback comparisons

The current generic fallback count is **186**. The removed six cases are the
three heights/two LODs for `NpcCivB` / `Market`, now intentionally registered

Both ordinary C++17 `-O2 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic` and focused ASan/UBSan builds
