# PlayerBody visual review

The final individual model is a substantial improvement over the saved generic
baseline, with a continuous shaped jaw, cheeks, nose and ear anatomy, seated
eyes, connected source fingers, dimensional clothing edges and a supported
strap/pouch. It preserves the rose shirt, green-gray trousers, chestnut bun,
dark sling and original shoes. It does not meet GTA VI or AAA visual quality.

Reviewed actual native GL pixels: 64 final 720×960 material/clay body/face
masters cover front, back, both profiles and all four obliques in Near and Far.
The immutable original executable produced 64 matched baseline views at the
same size, camera and lighting. PNG conversion was checked against the raw PPM
pixel bytes before compacting the raw captures; hashes and commands remain.
Contact-sheet resizing is for review only. Native masters remain unchanged.

The own face has a defined jaw turn and chin, a broad oval skull, recessed eye
seating and a continuous ear/neck transition. Its source-face difference audit
uses common source landmarks against all eight accepted faces. This establishes
independent geometry, not a claim of dramatic identity differences. Complete
interior mouth and exterior jaw follow Head; neck blending uses this actor's
source chart boundary. Both attention directions were inspected in the final
posed views. Generic height-only jaw weights are not used.

The long-sleeve silhouette, cuffs, fingers and shoe opening remain plausible
from both sides and the rear. The diagonal sling follows actual shirt facets
through the shoulder; the small pouch sits against the back with a supported
flap. Garment edges are dimensional. The front neckline was lowered and the
ankle opening fitted from exact triangle witnesses. The final full ordinary
contact matrix clears bind, idle, walking, running, opposite attention and
static crouch at three heights and both LODs.

Ninety-six additional final 480×640 posed masters show eight views of walking,
running, static crouch, crouch-walk and both extreme attention directions in
both LODs. Crouch-walk retains an obvious shared presentation limitation: the
high shin rotation and level shoe cause local ankle contact and a strained
leg silhouette; the deepest raised thigh also meets the shirt hem. Its strict
failure scope retains both contact regions. These stage poses use
height-scaled speeds; they are supplemented by the separately recorded real
9 m/s W and 19.8 m/s Shift production-input run and exact-state diagnostics.

Comparison against the inspected Rockstar and professional references informed
the continuous lid/socket/jaw construction, broad quiet cloth surfaces,
supported garment edges and gathered hair flow. The cap-supported hair and
rounded high rear knot improve the old block bun, but the cap remains smooth
and helmet-like at close range. Skin is smooth and low detail; the eyes lack
corneal refraction; the face and fingers have static art shaping rather than
expression or finger animation. Far simplifies hair, garment curvature and
hands. These are explicit remaining quality limits.

The 300-frame world comparison preserves scenery, the original spawn, HUD,
roaming people and their real occlusions. The synthetic InputState driver
enters the production Application frame chain before the camera and collision
update. Its world evidence is distinct from these neutral-stage diagnostics.
