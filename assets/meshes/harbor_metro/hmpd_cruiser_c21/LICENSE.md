# HMPD cruiser asset (Cycle 21)

`hmpd_cruiser_c21.blend` = the Cycle-20 HMPD cruiser (`../hmpd_cruiser_c20/`) with the base mesh's wheels and
brake discs deleted and replaced by original, procedurally modelled wheel assemblies
(`scripts/build_hmpd_wheels_c21.py`: 17x7.5 five-spoke alloy, P235/50R17 pursuit tyre with modelled tread blocks
and raised sidewall lettering, 320 mm ventilated cross-drilled/slotted rotor, two-piston caliper, dust shield).
The wheel geometry and materials are original work for Vaultline/Fury (no third-party wheel meshes).
